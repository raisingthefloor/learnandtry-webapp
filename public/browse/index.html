Mark, Gather, and Share Features

To make it easier to compare, save, and share the products that you find, we have added the following features:

  • Marking products of interest - You can mark any product that interests you by just clicking on the checkbox in the top-right corner of the product card.
  • Showing only Marked products - You can have all the marked cards gathered up and shown to you together, so you can explore and compare them more easily.
  • Copy (or Share with others) Link to Marked products - You can copy (or share with others) a link to the products that you marked.
    (If you or others later click on that link, it will launch the Learn & Try website and show just the products you marked.)
